Threads & mentions

Comment threads with @mentions, image attachments, and resolve states.

Annotated screenshots

Questions

Before you install.

Everything below is answered from how Marko actually ships today.

Is Marko really free?

Yes — free forever, open source under the MIT licence. There is no paid tier to upgrade to.

Do I need a Google account to sign in?

No. You sign up with an email and password. There is no Google or other third-party identity provider in the loop.

Does Marko track what I browse?

No. There is zero analytics and no tracking, and nothing leaves your device unless you choose to share it.

Do I have to host a backend?

No. Marko ships with a hosted backend, so you sign up and start reviewing — nothing to deploy or configure.

Can teammates see my reports?

Only the ones you invite. Projects are visible to their members alone, and pin screenshots are access-controlled so nobody outside the team can load them. Use Share to invite people by email; they sign up and see the same board.

Why does the Firefox install need repeating?

Firefox and Zen load the extension as a temporary add-on, and those are removed when the browser restarts. On Zen you can make it permanent with the optional about:config step in the install section.

What happens to a pin when the page changes?

Pins anchor to real page elements, so they survive scrolling, resizing, and reloads. Each one also stores a cropped screenshot of the element as it looked when you filed it.

Can I get the reports out of Marko?

Yes. Every project exports to PDF or CSV from the board in the web app.
