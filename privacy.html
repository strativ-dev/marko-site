Marko

Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Marko is a browser extension and web app from Strativ AB for pinning bug reports and UX feedback to live web pages. This page explains what Marko stores, where, and who can see it.

What Marko stores

  • Your account: email address, display name and, if you sign in with Google, your profile photo URL.
  • Your work: projects, pins (the page URL, element selector, comment, severity, status, assignee and due date), comment threads and @mentions.
  • Screenshots of the element you pin, captured only when you drop a pin.
  • Notifications about mentions, comments, invites and status changes.

Google sign-in

"Continue with Google" asks Google only for your email address and profile photo (the email and profile scopes). Marko never requests or accesses your Drive, Gmail, Calendar, Contacts or any other Google data. Your email identifies you to your team and matches project invites; your photo appears next to your pins and comments.

Where it is stored

  • Supabase hosts accounts, the database and screenshots. Row-level security means only members of a project can read them.

Notifications appear inside Marko only. The one email Marko sends is the invite to a new address, delivered by Supabase.

The extension keeps a signed-in session token in your browser's local extension storage so you stay signed in. It never holds your Google password.

Who can see your data

Only people invited to a project. If a project owner creates a public share link, anyone with that link can view that project read-only until the link is turned off. Exports (PDF and CSV) are files you create and share yourself.

What Marko does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone beyond the services above.
  • No reading of pages you are not actively reviewing.

Deleting your data

You can delete projects, pins and comments in the app at any time. To delete your account and everything attached to it, contact us and we will remove it.
